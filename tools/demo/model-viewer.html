Model viewer — interactive (orbit / zoom / pan)

The interactive analogue of renderModelStill: createModelViewer from @chestnutlabs/gcode-model-renderer, driving a source model through the shared interactive stage (DD-021). Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. The STL carries no material (neutral, materials: 'unavailable'); the synthetic 3MF declares real colors (materials: 'known'). Both inputs are fully synthetic and MIT-clean. window.viewerLoad('stl'|'3mf') and window.__viewer are exposed for the capture harness.
